Important benefits — a few things we did on purpose

Open the page, pick a room, and you have a plan. No account needed to try it.

Simple

We do one job: check that every room has a clear route to a door or window. Nothing extra in the way.

One job

Built for people who are not technical, and for anyone who wants a quick check of a home or office.

For everyone

Your photos and plans stay in your browser. We do not upload them anywhere unless you choose to share.

Private

Problems that we solve

Blocked exits go unnoticed

A wardrobe or sofa can block a door without anyone noticing. The route check flags it before it matters.

Plans are hard to make

Most people never write down how they would leave a room in an emergency. Ours takes about a minute.

Nobody wants a complicated app

Open it, pick a room, and start. No training needed.
