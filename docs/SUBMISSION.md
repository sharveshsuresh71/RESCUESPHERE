# Submission materials

## Title

RescueSphere

## Tagline

Your Room. Your Safety Plan. Your Preparedness.

## Short description

RescueSphere is a hands-first Mixed Reality preparedness-planning experience that helps households map a room, identify potential exits, review obstacles, compare user-defined routes, choose a meeting point, and save a private preparedness plan.

## Privacy

All user-created planning data is stored locally in the browser. RescueSphere does not require an account, collect GPS coordinates, collect precise home addresses, or upload private room layouts.

## Safety

RescueSphere is educational. It does not guarantee route safety, provide emergency-response instructions, or replace guidance from local authorities or emergency services.
