How it works

Everything in one plan.

RescueSphere is a planning aid for your home. Each step feeds the next, and your room data never leaves your browser.

Safety and privacy

Built to stay yours.

Your data, your device.

Room layouts, checklist progress and your meeting-point label are saved only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • No account needed to plan
  • Clear your browser data to remove everything
  • Works without sending your floor plan anywhere

A planning aid, not a guarantee.

RescueSphere is educational. It does not guarantee safety or replace emergency services and official local guidance.

  • Measure every dimension and exit in person
  • Follow local emergency authority guidance
  • Practise the drill with everyone in the home
